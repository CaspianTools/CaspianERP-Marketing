Pricing questions

Short answers to what people ask before they start.

Who counts as a user?
Anyone who can sign in to your workspace. People you keep records for without giving them a sign-in, such as contractors on your personnel register, do not count, and someone you suspend or offboard stops counting.
Is every module included?
Yes. The price per user covers every module, and there are no add-ons to buy. See the modules.
Is VAT included?
No. VAT is added at the rate that applies in your country.
Can we try it first?
Yes. Choose Try the demo on the sign-in page to open a sample company with no sign-up. It is shared with other visitors, so never enter real or personal data. For a walkthrough, request a demo.
What about migration and onboarding?
Tell us what systems and spreadsheets you use today. Migration, configuration, training and support are discussed with you before you start.
When is Enterprise the right choice?
When you need terms agreed with your organization, for example for a large number of users or several sites. Talk to us.
